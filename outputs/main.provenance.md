## Explicit uncertainty and falsifiers

- The assembled loop, initial two-mode policy and deferred extensions, optional planner, `brain.md` mediation, exact scheduling, item-validation pipeline, seven-table layout and Bun/Pi process topology are engineering/design choices. Their learning efficacy is untested. The starter fixed policy and time-matched delayed tests are evaluation recommendations, not a claim of benefit.
- Generated-item accuracy, difficulty, discrimination, equivalence of variants, and independent assessment validity require domain-specific review and empirical calibration. Source similarity or an LLM's answer does not validate an item. OCR, handwriting, speech, and open-ended scoring may fail and cannot silently gate advancement.
- The product's isolation and portability depend on version-specific Pi flags, resource-loader behavior, Bun compatibility, and platform tests; `PI_CODING_AGENT_DIR` alone is insufficient for strict isolation. Plugins have OS-level privileges unless a separate sandbox is implemented. The proposed package name/install command is deliberately a placeholder.
- A dose/time-matched trial showing improved independent delayed performance would strengthen the case for planner/adaptive policies. Repeated false mastery, item leakage, source inaccuracies, or no benefit over a simpler fixed tutor would weaken or reverse the recommendation.

## Subsequent scope and evidence revision

- An earlier revision broadened near-term preparation beyond exams/MCQs. The latest request retains that broad performance scope under **`/exam`** and starts with `/learn` plus `/exam` only. Essays, speeches, interviews and practical work use the actual performance criteria and observation limits; no universal rehearsal recipe or equally valid scoring across formats is claimed. The concept/component profiles illustrate math, English, science, humanities, languages, computing and creative/practical work; they are design examples, not a new effectiveness review for each subject.
- E12–E14 were added after Crossref metadata and publisher abstracts were checked: Koedinger, Corbett & Perfetti (2012), knowledge–learning–instruction framework, DOI `10.1111/j.1551-6709.2012.01245.x`; Koedinger & Aleven (2007), assistance dilemma, DOI `10.1007/s10648-007-9049-0`; Corbett & Anderson (1995), procedural knowledge tracing, DOI `10.1007/BF01099821`. These motivate behavior-specific instruction, qualified assistance and cautious learner modeling respectively. They do not validate this database ontology, exact component granularity, a universally optimal help rule, or cross-subject mastery/forgetting estimates. No new full-text replication or efficacy experiment was performed.
- The installed Pi extension type declarations were checked for `before_provider_request`, `after_provider_response` and `provider_stream_event`. Those declarations are not proof of complete provider/retry/compaction telemetry. `start.md` requires pinned-runtime coverage tests and labels unobservable fields as unknown; Bun never supplies a second model loop to fill instrumentation gaps.
- Immutable revisions and event/evidence rows are authoritative; temporal projections and maintenance jobs are mutable caches. Assessments and corrections pin rubric revisions and retain historical knowledge cutoffs. Assistance exposure and meaningful step attempts are first-class. These improve proposed audit/replay semantics; their learning benefit and practical burden still require testing.
- Subsequent learner feedback simplified the starter: context compilation is its own internal **Bun module**, not an agent/service/table; educational projections are deterministic folds with explicit time/sequence inputs; decision reasons accompany consequential domain events rather than every routine transition. Actual model-call instrumentation remains separate. The Pi tutor starts with the current target and an optional short working outline, not a mandatory planner-generated subject/prerequisite map. Generated links remain hypotheses and cannot justify broad skips. No new infrastructure, table or tool was added. These are single-learner engineering simplifications, not new research effectiveness claims.
