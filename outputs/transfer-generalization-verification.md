## Corrections and scope constraints

- Corrected Nunes et al. (2025) source to **Banerjee et al., “Children’s arithmetic skills do not transfer between applied and academic mathematics.”** Nature full text verifies *Nature* 639, 673–681 (2025), DOI 10.1038/s41586-024-08502-w; Figs. 1–2 and supplementary table locators added for the cited performance values.
- Pan & Rickard’s pooled *d*=.40 (95% CI [.31,.50]) is an average across heterogeneous transfer tests, **not a far-transfer estimate**. Do not overstate fine-grained categories beyond what the source/research sweep supports.
- Alfieri et al. overall *d*=.50 (95% CI [.44,.56]) is separate from the conditional subset with a supplied principle after comparison (*k*=29; near *d*=1.30, far *d*=.98; *p*=.20). The *d*=.98 estimate is **not** the overall far-transfer effect; publisher full text was inaccessible in this check.
- Final focused check retrieved Gentner et al.’s author PDF and checked Experiments 1–3/Tables 2–4. The paper explicitly reports 15/32 guided-analogy participants in Experiment 1 (the focused checker’s “solution cases” concern was not supported by the text); Experiment 2 counts and Experiment 3 dyad denominators are now stated in the review. The full study is still task-specific, not a broad transfer estimate.
- Retain Gick & Holyoak without exact Ns/effect estimates; direct primary PDF supports the qualitative account.
- Zhang & Zhao primary page supports the sample, design categories and task-level pattern; no standardized effect estimate was verified.
- Fuchs et al. primary paper supports the classroom details; it does not demonstrate cross-domain far transfer.
- Banerjee et al. results are observational field comparisons with task differences; do not infer a causal instructional effect or standardized far-transfer effect.
- van Blankenstein et al. primary paper supports the null PI-vs-SE transfer contrast. This is not proof of equivalence; the test was brief/immediate.
- Morris et al. is DOI-metadata level only in this check; keep it a narrow processing/test illustration.
- Perfetto et al. author PDF supports an access/spontaneous-use constraint, not absence of prior learning.
- Rittle-Johnson & Star: initial verifier pass could not independently establish DOI identity in a second index. Subsequent parent check matched title/DOI/journal/pages and abstract-level details in OpenAlex and ERIC; the primary full text was not retrieved, so the claim remains index/abstract-level and no effect estimates are included.
- NRC supports contextual framing; Bransford & Schwartz is not an intervention result; Sala et al. concerns cognitive training and is not an estimate of curriculum-transfer effects.
