# Research sweep notes — metacognition and study choice

Lead search used alphaXiv, PubMed, Crossref, OpenAlex, ERIC, web search, and direct full-text fetches. An intermediate researcher report informed the evidence map, but several bibliography details from that sweep were not accepted without cross-check. Private agent-session identifiers and paths are not published.

## Sources/claims carried into synthesis
- Rhodes & Tauber (2011), DOI 10.1037/a0021705: PubMed/Crossref identity checked; PubMed/search summaries report delayed JOL relative-accuracy meta effect g=.93 (112 effects, N=4,554), small memory reactivity g=.08 (98 effects, N=3,807); cue-only vs cue-target difference reported in directly fetched later paper.
- Yang et al. (2022), DOI 10.3102/00346543221094083: OpenAlex metadata/abstract, authors, year and title; 115 studies, 15,889 participants, 502 effects, non-intervention weighted within-person correlation .178. The construct is metacomprehension resolution, not calibration.
- Karpicke (2009), DOI 10.1037/a0017341: author-hosted PDF fetched and extracted; directly checked 4 experiments, one-week test, one-week retention contrast, and free-choice allocation in Experiment 2.
- Kornell & Son (2009), author-hosted PDF fetched and extracted; title/authors/venue and DOI 10.1080/09658210902832915 directly checked. Study-choice/belief results consulted; beliefs, choices, and performance treated separately.
- Karpicke (2013), DOI 10.3758/s13421-012-0274-5: PMC full text fetched; 120 students in Experiment 1, immediate vs one-day JOL conditions, cue-only vs cue-target, and judgment/retention reversal inspected.
- Tekin & Roediger (2021), DOI 10.1007/s11409-021-09260-0: PMC full text fetched; three paired-associate experiments and two-day outcomes checked; delayed JOLs not treated as equivalent to full overt retrieval.
- Ponce, Mayer & Méndez (2022), ERIC EJ1334669: record/abstract directly fetched; 36 articles, 85 effects; learner-generated and instructor-highlighted outcomes differentiated.
- Kobayashi (2005) DOI 10.1016/j.cedpsych.2004.10.001 and Kobayashi (2006) DOI 10.1080/01443410500342070: Crossref identity and ERIC abstracts; evidence differentiated between note-taking alone and note-taking-plus-review. Pooled estimates not reproduced because original tables not inspected.
- Dunlosky et al. (2013): Crossref identity and publisher/APS materials; utility ratings described as review judgments, not meta-analytic estimates.
- Yang et al. (2021), DOI 10.3389/feduc.2021.581216: full-text fetch attempted; extracted review/metadata and major heterogeneity/outcome caveats used.
- Dunlosky & Rawson (2012), DOI 10.1016/j.learninstruc.2011.08.003: Crossref identity plus ERIC/search result for the finding; exact N/effect not included.
- Tullis, Fiechter & Benjamin (2018), DOI 10.1037/xlm0000473: search abstract and PubMed/author-page links; five experiments, honored vs reversed test/restudy choices, report that honoring choices improves memory. Exact sample/time details not extracted; explicitly summary-level.
- Zhao et al. (2025), DOI 10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105279: researcher/database summary only; aggregate correlation r=.14 is labeled correlational and methods not full-text checked.

## Rejected or not relied on
- Researcher sweep's Kornell & Son DOI 10.1111/j.1467-9280.2009.02318.x conflicts with the fetched paper's own header (Memory 17(5), DOI 10.1080/09658210902832915); use the latter.
- Researcher sweep's Dunlosky & Rawson DOI 10.1037/a0026738 conflicts with Crossref title/author match for the 2012 Learning and Instruction paper, DOI 10.1016/j.learninstruc.2011.08.003; use Crossref/ERIC-matched DOI.
- Unverified Rowland (2014), Karpicke & Blunt (2011) and self-report percentages were not imported as quantitative claims.
- Semantic Scholar returned zero; OpenAlex anonymous API has limited quota; one PubMed query returned 429; some journal/APS pages were JavaScript-rendered. These were source-access/search limitations, not evidence that a claim is false.

**Status:** targeted broad evidence map, not a registered systematic review. Source verification is mixed across full texts, abstracts, and stable metadata; per-claim limitations remain in `outputs/evidence/07-metacognition.md`.
