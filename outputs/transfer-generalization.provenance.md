## Scope and method

Targeted literature review on transfer/generalization of learning. The search emphasized reviews/meta-analyses and primary empirical studies, with lab, classroom, medical-education, and field evidence kept distinct. Discovery and verification used alphaXiv/search, PubMed, Crossref, OpenAlex, ERIC, PMC, National Academies, publisher pages, and accessible author-hosted or institutional PDFs. Primary PDFs and article pages were retrieved for several central sources; not all primary full texts could be accessed. The review is not preregistered or exhaustive, and no new pooled analysis or effect-size calculations were performed.

Key scope decisions:
- Practiced-item retention is not counted as transfer by itself.
- “Near” and “far” are treated as source-specific labels. The review gives only an illustrative near-transfer heuristic, not a universal threshold or monotonic scale.
- Transfer dimensions and test validity are reported separately; cueing, delay, format, domain and context are not conflated.
- Cognitive-training synthesis is included only as adjacent boundary evidence, not as an estimate of curriculum or example-comparison effects.
- Instructional recommendations and proposed assessment are labeled as inferences, not observed trial outcomes.

## Unresolved limitations

1. No systematic risk-of-bias evaluation was conducted across every primary study or meta-analysis.
2. Alfieri subgroup results remain publisher-full-text-unverified and are labeled accordingly.
3. Gick & Holyoak study-specific N/effect estimates were not extracted; only qualitative results are reported.
4. Zhang & Zhao full text and exact delay were not verified in this pass.
5. Morris full article was not retrieved.
6. Some secondary conceptual sources (NRC; Bransford & Schwartz) provide framework rather than intervention evidence.
7. No author/lab corpus audit was requested or performed.

## Reproducibility / integrity

No calculations were performed and no new quantitative dataset was created. Quantitative statements in the review are transcribed from named source material or explicitly marked as not extracted. The proposed assessment design is a future experiment, not a completed result. Table effects are not directly compared across unlike designs or metrics. Direct source URLs and DOIs are listed in the review’s Key sources section.
